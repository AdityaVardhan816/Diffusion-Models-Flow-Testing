Section I: Problem Motivation

The Latency Bottleneck in Generative AI:
Why Traditional Diffusion is Slow

Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) have defined modern visual synthesis (Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 2). However, they impose a severe practical limitation: iterative multi-step sampling.

High Sampling Cost

Solving the reverse stochastic chain requires 50 to 100 sequential evaluations, incurring massive latency per image.

Flow Matching Shift

Continuous Normalizing Flows replace Markov noise schedules with deterministic probability transport along optimal vector fields.

The Project Mission

Compare both under strictly identical compute, then bridge them to achieve high-fidelity 1-step generation.

Section II: Theoretical Unification

Mathematical Formulation: SDE vs. ODE

Diffusion: Noise Estimation ($\epsilon$)

DDPM / DDIM

Clean data $x_1$ corrupted by Gaussian noise schedule $\bar{\alpha}_t$:

$x_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t} x_1 + \sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} \epsilon$

Objective trains network $\epsilon_\theta$ to predict injected noise vector:

$\mathcal{L}_{\text{diff}} = \mathbb{E}_{t, x_1, \epsilon} \left[ \|\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t) - \epsilon\|^2 \right]$

Reverse sampling requires solving stochastic SDE or probability flow ODE.

Flow Matching: Velocity Field ($v$)

1-Rectified Flow

Linear probability path between base noise $x_0$ and target $x_1$:

$x_t = (1 - t) x_0 + t x_1, \quad t \in [0, 1]$

Objective directly regresses constant velocity target $x_1 - x_0$:

$\mathcal{L}_{\text{CFM}} = \mathbb{E}_{t, x_0, x_1} \left[ \|v_\theta(x_t, t) - (x_1 - x_0)\|^2 \right]$

Reverse sampling is simple numerical ODE integration: $dx = v_\theta dt$.

Section IV: The 1-Step Failure

The Trajectory Crossing Problem:
Why 1-Rectified Flow Fails at 1 Step

When training 1-Rectified Flow, noise $x_0$ and data $x_1$ are sampled independently. Although each path $(1-t)x_0 + tx_1$ is straight, trajectories from different pairs intersect in state space.

Crossing Trajectories Introduce Curvature

Because paths cross, multiple target velocities exist at the same coordinate $x$. The neural network is forced to output the average velocity:

$v(x, t) = \mathbb{E}[x_1 - x_0 \mid x_t = x]$

This spatial averaging curves the marginal vector field. Taking a single large Euler step ($\Delta t = 1.0$) causes severe discretization truncation error.

1-RF at NFE = 1

Faint, washed-out smear ($\text{max} = 0.729, \sigma = 0.152$).

Section V: The Reflow Solution

Trajectory Straightening via 2-Rectified Flow (Reflow)

We bridge the paradigms by deterministically coupling noise and data endpoints, then retraining on these non-crossing transport lines.

Phase 1: Coupling Endpoints

Integrate 3,200 noise vectors $Z_0$ through the trained 1-RF model using 50-step high-accuracy ODE solver to produce destination endpoints $Z_1$:

$Z_1 = \text{ODE\_Solve}(v^1_\theta, Z_0, \text{steps}=50)$

Now $(Z_0, Z_1)$ are deterministically linked along the ODE flow.

Phase 2: Reflow Retraining

Train student model $v^2_\phi$ on the straight lines connecting these coupled endpoints:

$\mathcal{L}_{\text{reflow}} = \mathbb{E}[\|v^2_\phi(Z_t, t) - (Z_1 - Z_0)\|^2]$

Because paths do not cross, velocity becomes constant along each line ($v^2 \approx Z_1 - Z_0$).
